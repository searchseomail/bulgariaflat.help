Understanding Capital Gains Tax for Property Sales in Bulgaria

MistWalker
Topic Starter
Posted: 03.03.2025 08:26
I am currently looking into selling my apartment in Varna that I have owned for about three years. I am a bit confused about whether I am liable for capital gains tax in Bulgaria and what the current exemptions are for primary residences. Could someone clarify if there is a specific holding period before the sale becomes tax-free? Any advice or links to official government resources would be greatly appreciated.

In Bulgaria, you are generally exempt from capital gains tax if you have owned the property for more than three years, provided it is your only residential property. Since you've owned it for three years, you might be right on the edge, so check your notary act dates carefully.

According to the Bulgarian Personal Income Tax Act, the sale of one residential property is tax-free if you have owned it for more than three years. If you sell a second property, you are taxed on the profit. It's always best to verify your specific situation with the National Revenue Agency (NAP).
