Best family-friendly neighborhoods in Bulgaria for expats?

WildCard
Topic Starter
Posted: 06.01.2025 10:30
My family and I are planning to relocate to Bulgaria next year and we are currently trying to decide which city and neighborhood would be the best fit for us. We have two school-aged children, so proximity to international schools, parks, and safe playgrounds is our top priority. We are looking for a balance between a quiet residential feel and easy access to city amenities. Any recommendations or advice from those who have already made the move would be greatly appreciated.
