Investing in Bulgarian Real Estate for Residency - Process and Minimums?

SparkIgnite
Topic Starter
Posted: 03.01.2025 14:22
I am looking into the possibility of moving to Varna or Sofia and want to know how to get residency in Bulgaria by buying property. I have read that there is an investment threshold of 600,000 BGN, but I am unclear if this leads directly to a permanent permit or just a long-term stay. Does anyone have experience with the legal steps involved and if the property must be fully paid for before applying? I would also appreciate any recommendations for reliable lawyers who specialize in real estate immigration.

EmeraldEye
Replied: 19.01.2025 06:36
The 600,000 BGN threshold is indeed the requirement for a long-term stay (extended residency) under Article 24 of the Foreigners Act. It doesn't give you permanent residency immediately; you'll have to renew it annually for five years before you can apply for permanent status.
NeonNight
Replied: 31.01.2025 07:22
Yes, the property must be fully paid for and you need the Notary Act (title deed) showing the full amount. Mortgaged properties generally don't count toward the threshold for residency purposes.

I went through this last year. You definitely need a local lawyer because the bank transfers for such large sums are heavily scrutinized for AML (Anti-Money Laundering) compliance. Make sure your funds are ready and well-documented.

One thing to keep in mind is that the 600k BGN is per person if you are not married. If you're a couple, the legalities can get a bit more complex regarding ownership shares and residency rights.

Just a heads up, the 'investor' route is one of the more expensive ways. Many expats use the Trade Representative Office (TRO) route which is much cheaper, though it doesn't involve property ownership as the primary basis.

For Varna, I recommend looking at the 'Greek Quarter' or 'Seaside Park' areas. For Sofia, 'Lozenets' or 'Mladost' are popular. The process usually takes about 3-6 months from purchase to getting the actual ID card (Lichna Karta).
