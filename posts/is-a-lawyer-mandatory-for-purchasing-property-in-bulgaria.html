Is a lawyer mandatory for purchasing property in Bulgaria?

WanderLust_X
Topic Starter
Posted: 12.01.2025 03:58
I am currently looking at purchasing a holiday apartment in Bansko and have heard conflicting advice regarding the necessity of hiring a local lawyer. Is it possible to complete the transaction through just a notary and the real estate agency, or is legal representation essential to verify the title deeds? I want to ensure I am protected from any outstanding debts or legal encumbrances on the property. What are the common risks if I choose to proceed without independent legal counsel?

Technically, a lawyer is not mandatory by law, but it is highly recommended. The notary is impartial and only checks the documents provided, they don't do deep due diligence on potential debts or hidden liens.

Replied: 24.02.2025 13:07
Don't rely solely on the real estate agent. Their primary goal is to close the sale. A lawyer will verify the title deed history and ensure the property is free of encumbrances, which is crucial in Bansko.

The notary's role is limited to the act of transfer. They won't tell you if the seller has unpaid utility bills or if there is a pending legal dispute over the property. Definitely get a lawyer.
