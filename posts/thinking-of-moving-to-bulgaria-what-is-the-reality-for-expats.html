Thinking of moving to Bulgaria: What is the reality for expats?

SilverSling
Topic Starter
Posted: 19.01.2025 07:38
I am seriously considering relocating to Bulgaria for a remote work opportunity and a lower cost of living. While the scenery and tax benefits look appealing, I am concerned about potential language barriers and the cultural adjustment process. Could anyone who has already made the move share their honest pros and cons regarding daily life, bureaucracy, and integration? I would love to hear about your experiences with the local community and whether you feel it's a sustainable long-term choice.
