Is it safe to buy real estate in Bulgaria right now?

MysticRaven
Topic Starter
Posted: 06.04.2025 12:02
I have been looking at some apartments in Bansko and Sunny Beach recently, but I am worried about the current economic stability and legal protections for foreign buyers. I have read mixed reviews regarding maintenance fees and the complexity of securing a clean title deed in certain regions. Does anyone have recent experience with the Bulgarian market or advice on whether it is a risky time to invest given the current inflation? I want to make sure I am not walking into a legal nightmare or a potential property bubble.
