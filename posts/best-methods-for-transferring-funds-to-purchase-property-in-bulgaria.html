Best methods for transferring funds to purchase property in Bulgaria?

EmeraldEye
Topic Starter
Posted: 25.01.2025 13:00
I am currently in the process of buying an apartment in Varna and need advice on the most secure and cost-effective way to transfer a significant amount of money from my home country. I am concerned about bank fees, exchange rates, and the legal documentation required to prove the source of funds for the notary. Has anyone recently completed a property purchase in Bulgaria and could recommend a reliable service or bank for international wire transfers? Any tips on how to avoid excessive hidden charges would be greatly appreciated.

For high-value transfers, stick to SWIFT transfers between major banks. It might cost more in fees, but for real estate, you want the security of a direct bank-to-bank wire. Always keep the SWIFT confirmation document handy.

Do not use your personal bank's default exchange rate! They usually add a 2-3% margin. Use a dedicated service like OFX or Wise, but always inform your bank in Bulgaria in advance so they aren't surprised by a large incoming wire.
SonicBoom
Replied: 09.08.2025 00:16
When I bought my place, the notary asked for a 'Certificate of Origin of Funds.' Make sure you have your bank statements ready, translated into Bulgarian if necessary, to prove the money came from your salary or savings.
GoldenFalcon
Replied: 20.08.2025 06:22
I had a terrible experience with hidden fees using a traditional bank. They charged an intermediary fee that I wasn't told about. Always ask for a 'OUR' instruction on the wire transfer so you pay all fees upfront.
