Step by step guide to buying property in Bulgaria: Process and legal requirements?

NeonNight
Topic Starter
Posted: 03.01.2025 06:53
I am currently looking to invest in a vacation apartment along the Black Sea coast and find the Bulgarian real estate market quite attractive. However, I am feeling overwhelmed by the legal requirements, notary procedures, and the specific paperwork needed for foreign buyers. Could someone provide a comprehensive, step-by-step guide on the purchase process from reservation to the final title deed? I would also appreciate any advice on common pitfalls to avoid during the due diligence phase.

Buying in Bulgaria is straightforward but you definitely need a local lawyer. Start with a preliminary contract, pay the deposit, and then the notary handles the final deed. Make sure you check out is buying cheap property in Bulgaria actually worth it? before jumping in.

The process usually goes: reservation fee, preliminary contract (10% deposit), and then the Notary Act. Always verify the property has no encumbrances via the Property Register.
