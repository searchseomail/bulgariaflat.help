Where are the best places to buy real estate in Bulgaria for investment?

GhostProtocol
Topic Starter
Posted: 31.03.2025 07:42
I am currently exploring the Bulgarian property market and would love some local insights on where to invest. I'm trying to decide between a seaside apartment in Varna or a ski chalet in Bansko, but I'm also curious about the capital, Sofia. What areas currently offer the best balance of rental yields and potential for long-term capital growth? Any advice on the legal process for international buyers would also be greatly appreciated.
