Best websites to search for property in Bulgaria for foreigners?

ThunderBolt
Topic Starter
Posted: 04.01.2025 11:34
I am planning to move to Bulgaria next year and I'm currently looking for a reliable platform to browse real estate listings. There seem to be so many different portals, but I'm having trouble distinguishing which ones are actually updated and which ones are just outdated aggregators. Could you recommend the most trusted websites for finding apartments in major cities like Sofia or Plovdiv? Any tips on what to look out for to avoid scams would also be greatly appreciated.

Imoti.net and Alo.bg are definitely the go-to sites for locals, but they can be overwhelming if you don't speak Bulgarian. Use Chrome's auto-translate feature to navigate them effectively.

If you are looking for a place in Sofia, stick to Imot.bg. It is the most reliable portal. Before you commit to anything, make sure you check this guide on essential documents for property purchase in Bulgaria to avoid legal headaches.

Avoid any listings that look too good to be true or have stock photos. Always insist on a video call walk-through if you aren't in the country yet.

I highly recommend using professional agencies rather than individual listings on aggregators. Agencies like Address or Unique Estates are much more transparent for foreigners.
