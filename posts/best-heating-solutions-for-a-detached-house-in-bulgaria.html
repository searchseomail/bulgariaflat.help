Best heating solutions for a detached house in Bulgaria?

GravityZero
Topic Starter
Posted: 16.01.2025 13:43
I am currently looking for the most cost-effective heating options for a detached house in Bulgaria, considering the rising electricity prices. I am torn between installing a pellet boiler system, a modern heat pump, or sticking with traditional air conditioning units for the winter months. Could anyone share their experience with the efficiency of these systems during the harsh Bulgarian winters? Any advice on local maintenance availability or government subsidies for energy-efficient upgrades would be greatly appreciated.
