How to hire a reliable builder in Bulgaria

SonicBoom
Topic Starter
Posted: 28.01.2025 03:15
I am planning a full renovation of my apartment in Varna and am feeling overwhelmed by the process of finding a trustworthy contractor. I have heard many horror stories about local builders disappearing mid-project or using substandard materials. Does anyone have advice on how to vet professionals, verify their previous work, or navigate the local contract system? Any recommendations or tips on red flags to watch out for would be greatly appreciated.

Always ask for photos of past projects, but more importantly, ask to visit a site they are currently working on. Seeing the cleanliness of the workspace tells you a lot about their professional standards.

Never pay the full amount upfront. A standard practice in Varna is to split payments into stages. Only release the next payment once a specific milestone is completed and verified.

Red flag alert: If a builder pushes you to pay cash without a proper invoice or contract, walk away immediately. You need legal protection if things go sideways.

Word of mouth is your best friend here. Ask neighbors in your building if they have had work done recently. Varna is a small community, and bad reputations spread fast.
