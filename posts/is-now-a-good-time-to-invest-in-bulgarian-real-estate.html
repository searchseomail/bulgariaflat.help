Is now a good time to invest in Bulgarian real estate?

MysticRaven
Topic Starter
Posted: 12.01.2025 05:17
I have been considering diversifying my portfolio by purchasing a holiday apartment in Bansko or potentially a rental unit in Sofia. With the recent fluctuations in the European housing market and rising interest rates, I am concerned about whether Bulgarian prices are currently inflated. Has anyone here made a property purchase in Bulgaria recently, and what are your thoughts on the long-term rental yields? I would appreciate any advice on whether I should wait for a potential market correction or jump in now.
