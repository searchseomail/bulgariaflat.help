Tips for finding pet-friendly apartments in Bulgaria

PixelPerfect
Topic Starter
Posted: 31.03.2025 02:02
I am planning to relocate to Bulgaria with my dog and am currently struggling to find landlords who are open to tenants with pets. Most listings on popular platforms like Imot.bg don't explicitly mention pet policies, and I've already received several rejections after mentioning my golden retriever. Does anyone have advice on how to negotiate this or specific areas/agencies that are more accommodating to pet owners? Any tips on what to include in a 'pet resume' to help convince skeptical landlords would be greatly appreciated.

Finding a place with a dog in Bulgaria can be tough, but don't give up. Many landlords fear property damage, so offering an extra 'pet deposit' (usually half a month's rent) often changes their minds immediately.
UrbanNomad
Replied: 27.05.2025 03:20
I suggest looking at properties in best family-friendly neighborhoods in Bulgaria for expats because these areas tend to have more people with pets and landlords who are used to them.
PaperPlane

A 'pet resume' is a great idea! Include a photo of your dog, proof of vaccinations, and a reference from a previous landlord stating that your dog didn't cause any damage. It shows you are a responsible owner.

Replied: 07.08.2025 02:58
Avoid the big platforms like Imot.bg if you are struggling. Try joining Facebook groups for 'Expats in Sofia' or 'Rentals in Bulgaria.' People there are usually much more open to pets than the traditional rental agencies.
StaticShock
Replied: 14.08.2025 04:38
When you send a message to a landlord, don't just say 'I have a dog.' Explain that your dog is well-trained, house-broken, and that you are happy to sign a contract stating you will cover the cost of professional cleaning upon move-out.

Golden Retrievers are big dogs, which intimidates some landlords here. Maybe focus your search on apartments with a balcony or those located near a park so you can emphasize that the dog will get plenty of exercise outside.
