Best internet providers for rural homes in Bulgaria

SilverSling
Topic Starter
Posted: 15.02.2025 13:56
I am moving to a small village in the Rhodope Mountains and need reliable internet for remote work. Does anyone have experience with ISPs that service rural areas outside of the major cities? I am looking for something more stable than a standard 4G mobile hotspot if possible, so any recommendations for satellite or local wireless providers would be greatly appreciated.

In the Rhodopes, local WISP providers are your best bet. Look for small companies that use point-to-point microwave links rather than relying on cellular towers.

I lived in a village near Smolyan for a year. I used a local provider called 'RhodopeNet' and it was surprisingly stable, even during heavy snow.

Most villages in the Rhodopes have at least one local cable provider. Ask the neighbors, they usually know who provides the internet in the area.
