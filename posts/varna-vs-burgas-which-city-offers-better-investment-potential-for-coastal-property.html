Varna vs Burgas: Which city offers better investment potential for coastal property?

EmberEdge
Topic Starter
Posted: 12.01.2025 03:23
I am currently looking to purchase a holiday home on the Bulgarian coast and I am torn between Varna and Burgas. I want to know which city offers a better balance of rental yield, local infrastructure, and long-term appreciation. Are there specific neighborhoods in either city that expats should prioritize or avoid? Any advice from people who have recently bought in either location would be greatly appreciated.

Replies (11)

ThunderBolt
Replied: 10.03.2025 16:09
I've owned in Varna for five years now. It feels more like a year-round city than a resort town. If you want high rental yields, look near the Sea Garden or the Greek Quarter, but be prepared for higher entry prices. Definitely check out this guide to buying property in Bulgaria before putting down a deposit.
NeonNight
Replied: 01.04.2025 14:11
Burgas is currently seeing more industrial growth and port expansion, which is driving long-term appreciation. The Lazur neighborhood is the gold standard for expats—it's right next to the park and the beach. It feels much more laid back than Varna.
GoldenFalcon
Replied: 03.04.2025 08:22
Varna is definitely the cultural capital, but Burgas has better airport connectivity for international travelers. For a holiday home, I'd choose Burgas because the city center is more pedestrian-friendly and easier to navigate without a car.
EmeraldEye
Replied: 07.06.2025 15:51
Be very careful with 'new build' developments on the outskirts of either city. Many are poorly constructed. Always research the developer thoroughly and read up on common scams and red flags to watch out for before signing anything.
SolarFlare
Replied: 28.06.2025 03:39
I prefer Varna. The proximity to Golden Sands and St. Constantine and Helena means you have a much larger pool of seasonal renters if you decide to go the Airbnb route. Infrastructure is solid, though traffic in the center can be a nightmare in July.
DigitalDreamer
Replied: 16.07.2025 02:21
If you're looking for investment potential, Burgas is cheaper right now. You can get more square footage for your money in the Zornitsa or Bratya Miladinovi districts compared to Varna's expensive central areas.
UrbanNomad
Replied: 25.07.2025 06:21
I moved to Varna two years ago. The Greek Quarter is stunning but the old buildings often need expensive renovations. If you buy there, make sure to budget for plumbing and electrical upgrades, as those are the biggest headaches for expats.
GhostProtocol
Replied: 25.07.2025 07:39
Burgas has a fantastic vibe, especially around the Sea Garden. It feels less 'touristy' than Varna, which makes it better for a long-term holiday home. The rental market is steady because of the local university and port jobs.
ZenMaster
Replied: 27.08.2025 02:31
Don't ignore the smaller suburbs. In Varna, look at Vinitsa—it’s quieter, has great sea views, and is slowly becoming a favorite for expats who want to escape the city noise while staying close to the action.
PixelPerfect
Replied: 30.08.2025 06:11
I’m currently looking for financing and it’s tricky. Make sure you investigate all your mortgage options for non-residents in Bulgaria early on, as local banks have strict requirements for foreigners.
SilentStorm
Replied: 07.09.2025 14:33
Both cities are great, but Varna has a better 'city' feel during the winter months. If you want your holiday home to be usable in January, Varna won't feel like a ghost town, whereas some coastal areas in Burgas can get a bit quiet.
