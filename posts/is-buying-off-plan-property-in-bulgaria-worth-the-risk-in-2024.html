Is buying off-plan property in Bulgaria worth the risk in 2024?

RadiantRain
Topic Starter
Posted: 07.02.2025 01:33
I am currently looking into purchasing an off-plan apartment in a new development near Varna for investment purposes. While the lower entry price is very attractive, I am worried about potential construction delays or the developer going bankrupt before completion. Has anyone here had experience with Bulgarian developers, and what legal safeguards should I prioritize during the contract phase? I would appreciate any advice on whether the potential capital appreciation outweighs these inherent risks.

I bought off-plan in Varna three years ago. It was a stressful experience because the completion date was pushed back by nine months, but the capital appreciation was worth it in the end. My advice: hire an independent lawyer who doesn't work for the developer.
BlueHorizon
Replied: 09.05.2025 12:51
The risk is real. Many developers in Bulgaria rely on cash flow from new buyers to finish older projects. Before you sign anything, check if they have a history of completed projects and ask for bank guarantees.

Never pay the full amount upfront. Negotiate a payment schedule linked to specific construction milestones, like 'Act 14' (rough construction) and 'Act 15'. If the developer refuses this, walk away.

Make sure your contract is notarized and registered at the Registry Agency. This gives you a much stronger legal standing if the developer tries to sell the same apartment to someone else or goes bankrupt.

I work in construction in the Varna region. My tip: visit the site personally once a month. If you see construction activity slowing down, press the developer immediately. Do not rely on email updates.

The legal framework in Bulgaria has improved, but you must prioritize a 'preliminary contract' that clearly outlines the building specifications and materials. Developers often try to downgrade finishing materials to save money.
