Essential documents for property purchase in Bulgaria - what to prepare?

PaperPlane
Topic Starter
Posted: 22.01.2025 16:18
I am currently looking to purchase a holiday home in Bulgaria and I am a bit confused about the legal requirements for foreigners. Could someone provide a clear list of the specific documents I need to prepare to finalize the sale legally? I want to ensure I have everything in order before I start the conveyancing process with a local lawyer. Any advice on potential pitfalls or necessary certifications would be greatly appreciated.

Replies (8)

StaticShock
Replied: 08.02.2025 11:37
Congratulations on looking for a holiday home! You will primarily need your valid passport and, if you are buying as a company, the company registration documents. Ensure you have a lawyer check the Property Title Deed and the Tax Assessment Certificate for any outstanding debts.
IronGrit
Replied: 26.02.2025 04:30
The most important document is the 'Certificate of Absence of Encumbrances' issued by the Registry Agency. This proves the property isn't mortgaged. Also, don't forget to check for hidden costs of buying property in Bulgaria, as notary fees and local taxes can catch you off guard.
WildCard
Replied: 29.07.2025 04:42
If you are a non-EU citizen, you might face restrictions on owning land, which is why many foreigners set up a Bulgarian company. Your lawyer will guide you through this, but definitely prepare your passport and proof of funds early on.
VelvetVortex
Replied: 18.08.2025 16:14
Always ask for the 'Scheme of the Property' (cadastral map) to ensure the boundaries are correct. I learned the hard way that land disputes are common in some areas; definitely read up on the pros and cons of moving to rural Bulgaria before signing anything.
CrimsonNova
Replied: 04.10.2025 04:57
You need to prepare a Power of Attorney if you cannot be present for the signing. Make sure it is notarized and apostilled if you are signing from abroad. Your lawyer should handle the 'Declaration of Non-Indebtedness' to the state, which is mandatory.
SonicBoom
Replied: 10.11.2025 14:01
Don't forget the 'Energy Performance Certificate'. It is now required by law for all property sales in Bulgaria. It's a small detail, but you can't finalize the deal at the notary without it.
CobaltCrush
Replied: 21.12.2025 02:17
Make sure you have your bank statements ready for the source of funds check. Anti-money laundering regulations are quite strict here, so transparency is key to avoiding delays during the conveyancing process.
NeonNight
Replied: 26.01.2026 01:45
Hire an independent lawyer who doesn't work for the developer or the seller. They need to verify the 'Act 16' (Usage Permit) for apartments, as this is crucial for utility connections and legal occupancy.
