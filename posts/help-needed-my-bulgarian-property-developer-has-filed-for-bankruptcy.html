Help needed: My Bulgarian property developer has filed for bankruptcy

QuantumQuill
Topic Starter
Posted: 08.03.2025 10:00
I purchased an off-plan apartment in Bansko three years ago, but I just received notice that the development company has declared insolvency. Construction has stalled at the grey shell stage, and I am worried about losing my full investment. Does anyone have experience with the Bulgarian insolvency process or know if there is a way to reclaim my funds or secure the title? Any advice on finding a reputable local lawyer specializing in these types of real estate claims would be greatly appreciated.

I am so sorry you are going through this. I went through a similar situation in Bansko back in 2012. You absolutely need to hire a local insolvency lawyer immediately to file a claim in the commercial register. Do not wait, as the window to register your debt is limited.

Check your preliminary contract to see if you have a 'Notary Act' already. If you don't, you are technically just a creditor, and you will be at the back of the line behind the banks. You need a lawyer who specifically knows the Bulgarian Commercial Code.

The 'grey shell' stage is the worst because the property isn't legally habitable yet. You need to verify if the developer actually owned the land or if it was leased. That changes everything regarding your claim priority.
