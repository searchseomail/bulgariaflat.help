Monthly utility expenses for a standard 2-bedroom apartment in Sofia?

QuantumQuill
Topic Starter
Posted: 19.04.2025 12:49
I am planning to move to Bulgaria soon and am trying to budget for my monthly living expenses. Could someone provide a realistic breakdown of utility costs, including electricity, heating, water, and internet for a standard 60-70 sqm apartment? I am particularly curious about the seasonal difference between summer and winter heating bills. Any tips on how to keep these costs manageable would be greatly appreciated.

For a 60-70 sqm apartment in Sofia, expect to pay around 150-200 BGN in the summer and up to 350-450 BGN in the winter if you rely on central heating (Toplofikatsiya). If you are looking for long-term rental advice, check these recommendations for reliable real estate agencies in Bulgaria for expats to ensure you find a place with good insulation.
AlphaOmega
Replied: 21.05.2025 13:37
Electricity is the biggest variable. Heating with AC units is usually cheaper than central heating if you have a modern inverter. In summer, my electricity bill is about 60 BGN, but in deep winter, it jumps to 150 BGN. Water is usually a flat rate per person, around 15-20 BGN.
ThunderBolt
Replied: 29.05.2025 15:15
Internet and TV packages are surprisingly affordable here, usually costing between 20-35 BGN per month for high-speed fiber. Always check if the building has fiber optics installed before signing your lease.

Don't underestimate the cost of winter heating. Older buildings without exterior insulation can be very expensive to heat. I live in a 65 sqm apartment in Lozenets. In winter, my heating bill is the main expense. I suggest buying a smart thermostat if you use electric radiators to keep the costs down when you are away from home.
LunarEcho
Replied: 24.01.2026 05:30
Water is cheap, but communal building maintenance fees (vhod) can add up to 20-40 BGN per month depending on if there is a lift or a professional cleaning service. Always ask the landlord about the 'vhod' fees upfront.
VelvetVortex
Replied: 25.01.2026 09:46
The seasonal difference is significant. Winter heating can triple your utility bill compared to the summer months. If you are still in the process of looking for a flat, make sure to inspect the windows—double glazing makes a massive difference in your monthly budget.
DigitalDreamer
Replied: 30.01.2026 06:48
Budgeting for utilities is straightforward once you know the basics. Just keep in mind that central heating bills are calculated based on consumption and common parts, so they can be a bit unpredictable if the building isn't energy efficient.
