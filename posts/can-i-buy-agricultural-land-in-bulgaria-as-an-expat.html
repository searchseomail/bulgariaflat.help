Can I buy agricultural land in Bulgaria as an expat?

DataDrifter
Topic Starter
Posted: 26.01.2025 15:17
I am looking into investing in a small plot of rural land in Bulgaria for hobby farming, but I am confused about the current ownership laws for non-EU citizens. I have heard conflicting information regarding whether I need to register a Bulgarian company to complete the purchase or if I can buy it as an individual. Could someone clarify the legal requirements and any specific restrictions I should be aware of before starting the process?

Replies (11)

SilverSling
Replied: 01.02.2025 14:02
As a non-EU citizen, you generally cannot buy agricultural land in your own name in Bulgaria. The standard procedure is to register a local Bulgarian company, which then acts as the legal entity purchasing the land.
StaticShock
Replied: 12.02.2025 03:56
I went through this process last year. You definitely need to set up a limited liability company (OOD) to acquire agricultural plots. It’s not too expensive, but make sure you read up on the legal requirements for non-EU citizens before signing anything.

If you are from a country with a reciprocal agreement with Bulgaria, the rules might differ slightly, but for 99% of non-EU expats, the company route is the only viable path for farmland.
