Navigating the legal requirements for renting out a holiday apartment in Bulgaria

ZenMaster
Topic Starter
Posted: 18.01.2025 10:30
I recently purchased a vacation property in Sunny Beach and am considering listing it on platforms like Airbnb to cover my maintenance costs. I am confused about the registration process with the local municipality and the specific tax obligations for non-resident owners. Does anyone have experience with the process of obtaining a category rating for a private property and how to handle the income tax declaration properly? Any advice on local regulations or recommended legal contacts would be greatly appreciated.

Congratulations on the purchase! You definitely need to register the property as a 'tourist accommodation' at the local municipality in Nesebar. It's a bureaucratic hurdle, but mandatory for legal Airbnb operations.

Replied: 17.04.2025 10:43
Don't skip the categorization process. You need to apply for a star rating, which involves an inspection. Many owners try to bypass this, but the fines are getting quite hefty lately.

As a non-resident, your rental income is subject to a flat tax rate. You must file an annual tax return in Bulgaria. I highly recommend consulting a local accountant who specializes in expat properties.

I went through this in Sunny Beach last year. You need a document called a 'Categorization Certificate'. You can apply at the mayor's office, but be prepared for a bit of paperwork regarding fire safety standards.
