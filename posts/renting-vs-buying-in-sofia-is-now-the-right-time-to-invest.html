Renting vs. Buying in Sofia: Is now the right time to invest?

PixelViper
Topic Starter
Posted: 13.01.2025 13:12
I have been living in Sofia for three years and I am currently torn between continuing to rent or taking out a mortgage for a place of my own. Prices in popular areas like Lozenets and Manastirski Livadi have skyrocketed recently, making me wonder if the market is currently overvalued. On the other hand, monthly rents are also rising steadily, and I feel like I might be missing out on long-term equity. What are your thoughts on the current real estate climate in Bulgaria, and is it still possible to find good value for money?

I've been in Sofia for five years and honestly, the 'bubble' talk has been going on since 2018. If you are planning to stay for the next 5-10 years, buying is almost always better than paying someone else's mortgage. Just be very selective about the building quality.
