I am currently looking at purchasing an apartment in Sofia, but the listings keep mentioning Act 14, 15, and 16. Could someone please clarify what these stages actually represent in terms of legal ownership and building completion? I want to make sure I understand the risks involved if I buy a property that has not yet reached Act 16. Any advice or experiences with these milestones would be greatly appreciated.

Act 14 means the rough construction is finished (roof and walls). It is the stage where you can legally sign a preliminary contract, but wait until Act 16 to move in. If you are debating the market, check out this guide on Renting vs. Buying in Sofia before making a final decision.

Act 15 is the 'acceptance of the building' where all technical installations are tested. It’s safer than Act 14, but you still aren't fully legal to reside there. Always hire a lawyer to check the developer's history.
PixelPerfect

Act 16 is the 'Permission for Use.' Without this, you officially don't have a habitable property. Utilities like electricity and water are often charged at industrial rates until this is granted.

Act 14 is just the skeleton of the building. Act 15 is when the commission inspects everything. Act 16 is the final stamp. Never pay the full amount before Act 16 is issued.

The legal ownership transfers via a Notarial Deed. You can technically sign this at Act 14, but you become responsible for the property before it's even finished. Be very careful with the contract terms.
