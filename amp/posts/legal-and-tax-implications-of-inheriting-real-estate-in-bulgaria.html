Inheriting property in Bulgaria as a foreigner is definitely a bureaucratic maze. You will need to obtain a Certificate of Heirs from the local municipality first. It is highly recommended to hire a local lawyer because the land registry documents are all in Bulgarian and the legal procedures are quite specific.
