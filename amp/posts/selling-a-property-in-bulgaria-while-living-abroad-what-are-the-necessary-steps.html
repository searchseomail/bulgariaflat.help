I own an apartment in Sunny Beach but moved back to the UK several years ago and no longer have the time to visit. I am looking to sell the property, but I am unsure how to handle the legal paperwork and power of attorney from outside the country. Do I need to fly back to Bulgaria to finalize the sale, or can this be managed entirely through a local lawyer? You definitely don't need to fly back. I sold my place in Bansko while living in London. You just need to hire a local lawyer and grant them a Power of Attorney (PoA). It needs to be notarized and likely apostilled in the UK before being sent to Bulgaria.
