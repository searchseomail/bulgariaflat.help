I am currently looking into purchasing a holiday home in the Burgas region, but I am unsure about the specific legal steps for foreigners. Does a foreign individual need to register a company to own the land, or is that only for specific types of property? I am also curious about the average closing costs and how to find a trustworthy lawyer to handle the title deed transfer. As a non-EU citizen, you can own the building itself in your own name, but you cannot own the land it sits on. To own the plot, you must register a Bulgarian Limited Liability Company (OOD or EOOD). It's a standard procedure and usually costs around 300-500 EUR in legal fees.

Closing costs typically range from 3% to 5% of the purchase price. This includes the local tax (which varies by municipality), notary fees, and the entry fee at the Registry Agency. Budget for a bit more if you are setting up a company too.

The 'Act 16' is the most important document you need to look for. It's the final building permit that confirms the property is fit for habitation. Don't be tempted by 'Act 14' or 'Act 15' properties unless you really know what you are doing, as they might lack electricity or water connections.
