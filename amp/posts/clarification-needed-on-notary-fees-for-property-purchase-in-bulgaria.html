ZenMaster OP
01.01.2025 11:59
I am currently in the process of purchasing an apartment in Varna and have received conflicting information regarding the official notary fees. Could someone explain how these fees are calculated based on the property's material interest and if there are additional local taxes I should be prepared for? I want to ensure I have enough liquidity set aside for the closing process without any surprise costs at the notary's office. Any advice on how to verify these amounts before the final deed signing would be greatly appreciated.

Notary fees in Bulgaria are strictly regulated by the Notary Tariff based on the 'material interest' (the property price). You can use an online notary fee calculator to get a rough estimate, but always add 20% VAT to the base fee.
