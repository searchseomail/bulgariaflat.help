I am currently looking at several apartments in the Bansko area and the initial listing prices seem very affordable compared to the UK. As a general rule, you should budget about 4% to 6% of the purchase price for the total closing costs. This includes the municipal tax (usually around 3%), notary fees which follow a state-defined scale, and the registration fee at the Registry Agency. Bansko is quite affordable, but these percentages are fixed regardless of the area.

Be very careful with maintenance fees in Bansko. Many apartment complexes there have mandatory annual fees ranging from 8 to 15 Euros per square meter. If the complex has a pool and spa, it will be on the higher end. Some owners are currently in legal battles over these fees, so always ask to see the existing maintenance contract before signing anything.

Don't forget the real estate agency commission. While some sellers pay it, in many cases, the buyer is expected to pay a 3% commission plus 20% VAT. Also, legal fees for an independent lawyer—which I highly recommend—will set you back another 500 to 1,000 Euros depending on the complexity of the deal.

Translation costs are unavoidable for foreign buyers. You are legally required to have a licensed translator present at the notary signing if you don't speak Bulgarian. They usually charge by the hour, and you'll also need to pay for the written translation of the Title Deed (Notary Act). Budget around 150-200 Euros for this.

One 'hidden' cost people often overlook is the change of utility accounts. After you get the Notary Act, you have to register the property in your name at the tax office, and then change the names on the electricity (EVN) and water (V&K) accounts. If you hire someone to do this for you, it usually costs about 100-150 Euros.

The municipal tax is the biggest chunk. In Bansko, it's currently 3% of the purchase price or the tax evaluation price, whichever is higher. Be aware that some sellers might suggest declaring a lower price to save on taxes, but this is risky and generally not advised for foreign buyers as it can cause issues with proof of funds later.

Annual property taxes are actually very low in Bulgaria compared to the UK. For a standard one-bedroom in Bansko, you're looking at maybe 50-80 Euros a year for property tax and garbage collection fees combined. The real 'killer' is the complex maintenance fee, not the government taxes.
