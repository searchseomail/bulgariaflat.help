Always hire a local 'brigada' (work crew) but keep a strict eye on them. If you aren't there to supervise daily, work will likely stop or materials will disappear. Never pay the full amount upfront.

Plumbing and electrical are tricky in rural areas. Ensure you hire a certified electrician, as old Bulgarian wiring is a fire hazard. Many village houses use lead pipes that definitely need replacing.
